Frequently Asked Questions About Chotu

Everything you need to know about Chotu's QR ordering and restaurant management platform.

What is Chotu? +

Chotu is a QR ordering and restaurant management platform that helps restaurants manage digital menus, table orders, kitchen operations, billing and payments from one platform.

Do customers need to install an app to place an order? +

No. Customers can scan the restaurant's table QR code using their phone camera and open the digital menu without downloading an app.

How does QR ordering work in a restaurant? +

Guests scan the QR code at their table, open the digital menu, select items and place their order. The order is sent to the kitchen display with the table information so staff can prepare and serve it.

Does Chotu provide a digital menu? +

Yes. Chotu provides a digital restaurant menu that customers can open directly from the table QR code. Restaurants can showcase categories, photos and item availability.

Can Chotu send orders to the kitchen? +

Yes. Orders placed through the QR ordering system are shown on the kitchen display along with the table information, helping restaurant teams reduce order mix-ups.

Does Chotu support restaurant billing? +

Yes. Chotu supports restaurant billing and WhatsApp invoicing, allowing teams to generate bills and share invoices with customers.

Does Chotu charge commission on orders? +

Chotu currently offers 0% commission on orders. The pricing plans are subscription-based, with no commission on orders.

How much does Chotu cost? +

Chotu's Starter plan is ₹999 per month and the Growth plan is ₹2,499 per month. Enterprise pricing is customized for multi-outlet restaurants and franchises.
